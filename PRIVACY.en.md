# HawkView Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-18 · Korean original: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) (the Korean text governs if they differ)

"The app" means HawkView, an image viewer that looks and works the same on Mac and Windows. "We" means Youngjoon Kim, who makes and distributes the app. This policy covers the app, the app site (https://hawkviewer.app) and the support email.

## 1. In short

- What the app handles (the images and archives you open and their contents, folder paths, settings, recent files, ratings and tags, thumbnail cache) never leaves your device. All processing happens on your device.
- The app has no ads, no usage tracking and no analytics. There is no account or sign-up.
- The app uses the network only in the cases listed in section 2.
- The only personal information we receive is what you send us yourself (section 3).

## 2. When the app uses the network

- If you click the map button (showing a photo's GPS location), the app opens a map service in your default browser. From then on that service's own policy applies.
- If you click the privacy, terms or support links in the About window, the app opens our site in your default browser.

### Update check

The Mac version checks once a day (the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store). The app asks https://hawkviewer.app/appcast.xml whether a new version exists.

- The request carries your IP address, the app version and the OS version, as any internet request does.
- We only count requests per day. We do not store IP addresses.
- The request and the update file pass through Cloudflare and GitHub, which handle their access logs under their own policies (section 5).
- You can turn automatic checks off: in Settings › Updates, turn off "Download new versions in the background and switch on quit".

## 3. Personal information we process

| What | Why | When we receive it | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support email: email address, name (if you give it), message and attachments | To answer you and fix problems | When you email us | One year from the day we finish replying |

We do not receive anything else. Please do not put unnecessary personal information (national ID numbers, other people's data) in an email; if we receive it, we delete it.

## 4. We do not give it to others

We do not sell or give your personal information to anyone. The only exception is a request from an authority that the law requires us to meet.

## 5. Service providers and transfers outside Korea

We use the services below. All of these companies are outside Korea, so using the service sends information abroad.

| Recipient | Country | What it does | Information sent | When and how | Policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare, Inc. | United States | Forwards support email (Email Routing); serves the site and update-check requests | Email address and content (forwarded, not stored), IP address | When you email us or visit the site or update address, over the network | https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/ |
| Google LLC (Gmail) | United States | Stores and answers support email | Email address, name (if given), message and attachments | When we receive your email, over the network | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| GitHub, Inc. | United States | Serves installers and update files | IP address, app version | When you download or update, over the network | https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement |

If you do not want your information to go abroad, simply do not email us; we will then be unable to answer you. Downloading and using the app works as before.

## 6. Deletion

We delete information as soon as the retention period ends. Email is permanently deleted from the mailbox, and files are erased so they cannot be recovered. Records we must keep by law are kept separately and deleted when that period ends.

## 7. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop its processing. Email support@hawkviewer.app. We will respond and act without undue delay. A legal representative or an authorized agent may also make the request.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 without a legal guardian's consent. If an email turns out to come from a child, we obtain the guardian's consent or delete the email.

## 8. How we protect it

- Two-step verification is on for the mailbox and service admin accounts.
- Downloaded material is kept only on encrypted disks.
- Passwords and keys live in the operating system keychain, never in files or code.
- One person, the developer, handles personal information.

## 9. Cookies

The app uses no cookies or similar devices. The app site uses none either.
